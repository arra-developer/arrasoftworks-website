Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This policy explains how Arra Softworks ("Arra", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal data when you visit arrasoftworks.com or contact us through it. We serve clients worldwide and aim to meet the data protection laws that apply to them, including the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where it applies.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Arra Softworks is the data fiduciary (data controller) for personal data collected through this website. For any privacy question or request, email developer@arrasoftworks.com.

2. What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Name, email address and your messageWhen you submit the contact formTo reply to your enquiry and discuss a possible project

We do not ask for sensitive data and do not knowingly collect data from children under 18.

3. Legal basis

We process contact-form data on the basis of your consent, given when you choose to send the form, and to take steps you request before entering into a contract. We process analytics data only on the basis of your consent, given through the cookie banner. You can withdraw consent at any time (see section 7).

4. Service providers we use

We share data only with providers that help us run the website. They process it on our behalf:

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co): forwards contact-form submissions to our inbox.
  • Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC): website analytics. It uses cookies such as _ga and _ga_* to distinguish visitors. Google may process data outside your country.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.): hosts the website and may log IP addresses for security.
  • Fontshare and Google Fonts: serve the fonts used on the site; your browser sends them your IP address when loading fonts.
  • Our email provider: stores the enquiries we receive.

We do not sell your personal data or use it for third-party advertising.

5. International transfers

Some providers above store or process data in other countries, including in the United States. Where this happens, we rely on the safeguards those providers offer and transfer data only as permitted under applicable law.

6. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and related emails: up to 24 months after our last contact, unless you become a client, in which case we keep records as needed for the engagement and legal obligations.
  • Analytics data: up to 14 months in Google Analytics.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you, or get a summary of it;
  • correct, complete or update it;
  • erase it;
  • withdraw consent you have given;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights (DPDP Act).

Email developer@arrasoftworks.com and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the data protection authority in your country.

8. Cookies and analytics choices

Google Analytics cookies are set only if you click "Accept" in the cookie banner. If you decline, or make no choice, Google Analytics does not load. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings; withdrawing consent stops analytics and removes its cookies. Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. The site works without these cookies.

9. Security

The site is served over HTTPS, and we limit access to enquiries to the people who need them. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services or the law change. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.

Questions? Email developer@arrasoftworks.com.
