# Arra Softworks: Website Content (mapped to the template)

This file maps Arra Softworks copy onto every text slot in `index.html`, section by section and in page order.

- **Slot:** the element in the template.
- **Max:** approximate character limit for the slot, spaces included, measured against the live template. Going over usually means an extra line or a broken layout.
- **✱** marks copy drafted from the brief to fill a slot the brief didn't cover. Review it before going live.
- **[TBD]** marks a slot that needs real information only the client has, such as names, numbers, quotes or links.
- **Alt:** an optional second headline to choose from.

---

## Voice & style notes

- **Plain-spoken and confident.** Say what we do and what the client gets. Short sentences, active verbs, "we" and "you".
- **Founder-friendly, not salesy.** Write like a senior engineer who also understands fundraising. Avoid buzzwords such as "cutting-edge", "end-to-end solutions", "leverage", "synergy" and "world-class".
- **No unproven claims.** No numbers, client names, quotes or awards unless they're real. Soft positioning is fine. Stats are not.
- **Capitalization:** Title Case for the big display headings (H1/H2, timeline card titles, "Our Work"). Sentence case for everything else: eyebrows, buttons, pills, list items, FAQs and table cells.
- **Parallel lists:** within a list, use one form throughout. Checklist items start with a verb ("Scope the MVP"). Service lists are noun phrases ("API and backend engineering"). Bubbles are all questions.
- **Consistent terms:** "D2C and B2B brands", "founders", "go-to-market" (hyphenated), "iOS, Android and web", "fundraising". No serial comma. Use "and" rather than "&" in body copy. "&" is fine only in tight labels such as table rows and the ticker.
- **Handwritten notes:** lowercase, casual, at most 2 short lines, one exclamation mark at most.

## 8. FAQs `#faq`

✱ All 12 are drafted from the brief and make no claims beyond it. Questions show on one line, so keep them to about 55 characters. Answers should stay under 200.

1. **What does Arra Softworks do?** We build mobile and web apps for D2C and B2B brands, and work with founders as a technical and strategic partner, from MVP to a fundable, scalable business.
2. **Who do you work with?** D2C and B2B companies that need a product built and shipped, and early and mid-stage founders who need a partner beyond code.
3. **What platforms do you build for?** iOS, Android and the web, plus the APIs and backend that power them.
4. **Can you build our MVP?** Yes. We scope the MVP around your problem, users and constraints, then build it in short cycles with your feedback in the loop.
5. **Do you only write code?** No. For founders we also cover security, scaling, cost optimization, go-to-market, sales enablement and fundraising.
6. **How does a project run?** Scope, build, launch, grow. We start with your problem, ship working software in short cycles, launch with monitoring and stay on if you need us.
7. **What happens after launch?** You get deployment, monitoring, a handover you can maintain and post-launch support. Founder clients can keep us on for scaling and growth.
8. **Can our team maintain the product ourselves?** Yes. Every build ends with a handover your team can own.
9. **Can you review our security and infrastructure?** Yes. Security and infrastructure reviews are part of our founder services.
10. **Can you help cut our cloud and performance costs?** Yes. We review performance and infrastructure, then optimize for cost.
11. **Do you help with fundraising?** Yes: pitch deck and investor narrative, investment readiness and support through the raise.
12. **How do we get started?** Send us a note through the contact form below. Tell us what you're building and what stage you're at.
